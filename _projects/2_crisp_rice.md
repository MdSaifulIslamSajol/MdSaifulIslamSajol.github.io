**CRISP-RICE: Climate Resilient Innovations for Sustainable Production of Rice (USDA-NIFA grant) · Jul 2025 – Present** · [Project page (USDA National Agricultural Library)](https://www.nal.usda.gov/research-tools/food-safety-research-projects/climate-resilient-innovations-sustainable-production-rice-crisp-rice)

#### About the project

CRISP-RICE is a multi-state, multi-institutional, and multidisciplinary project funded by the USDA Sustainable Agricultural Systems program. The overarching goal is to enhance the profitability and sustainability of rice production in the Southern United States while addressing food security challenges under changing climatic conditions. The project is led by Principal Investigator Dr. Prasanta K. Subudhi, with Dr. Supratik Mukhopadhyay serving as Co-Principal Investigator.

The project combines expertise across plant breeding, genetics, agronomy, soil science, engineering, economics, and AI to design sustainable and resilient agricultural practices. As part of this federally funded initiative, I contribute to research that integrates artificial intelligence, data modeling, and precision agriculture to develop climate-smart rice production systems. My contributions involve data-driven modeling and analytical frameworks that support sustainable decision-making in AI-assisted rice breeding, with a focus on environmental impact assessment, climate resilience, and scalable AI-enabled agricultural solutions.

#### Goal of my work

Drought is one of the biggest threats to rice yield, and a gene's response to drought can differ between rice varieties, tissues, and growth stages. Our goal is to build machine learning tools that predict how rice genes respond to drought in a given biological setting, and, in the longer term, to help design regulatory DNA for crops that cope better with climate stress.

#### What we are doing

- **Predicting drought-responsive gene regulation.** We are developing deep learning models that predict whether a rice gene is up- or down-regulated under drought, using its promoter DNA together with biological context such as variety, tissue, and developmental stage. This work includes my M.Sc. thesis and two first-author manuscripts that are currently under review (CARCM and a workshop paper at IEEE BigData 2026).
- **Generative design of regulatory DNA.** With CisGPT (first author, in preparation), we are developing a generative AI model for designing regulatory DNA sequences, and testing whether the approach carries over to other organisms.
- **Careful evaluation.** We compare our models against established machine learning baselines and recent DNA foundation models on the same data, so that each improvement is measured rather than assumed.

#### What comes next

- Completing peer review and publishing the manuscripts, then releasing code and models openly.
- Extending the work to other environmental stresses and crops.
- Working with plant science collaborators on laboratory validation of designed sequences, which has not been done yet.

Details of the methods and results will be shared here once the papers are published.

This project reflects a commitment to interdisciplinary research that bridges AI and agricultural sustainability to address global food security challenges.
