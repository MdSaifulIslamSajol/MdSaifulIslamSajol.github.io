Ph.D. student at Louisiana State University (expected July 2027) working as a Graduate Research Assistant under Dr. Supratik Mukhopadhyay. I work with artificial intelligence and machine learning, including deep learning, agentic AI, and large language models, across computational biology and bioinformatics, drug discovery, computer vision, and wildfire detection and prevention using space-based sensor technology, with a focus on reliable, openly released research software.
