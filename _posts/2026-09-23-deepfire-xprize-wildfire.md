The LSU **DeepFire** team received the **$100,000 Special Recognition for Operational Deliverable** in the Wildfire XPRIZE, Space-Based Wildfire Detection and Intelligence Track. DeepFire was one of five awarded teams in the track, among more than 300 teams worldwide. XPRIZE described the recognition as being for "an operational fire-spread-alert capability designed to support wildfire detection and intelligence."

DeepFire links models for fire-risk prediction, early fire detection, and fire spread forecasting. My contributions are machine learning models that integrate meteorological data, satellite imagery, smoke detection, and spatio-temporal fire spread modeling, and data fusion pipelines that combine predictive risk maps with real-time environmental signals.
