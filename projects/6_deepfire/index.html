DeepFire, AI for Wildfire Prediction

LSU multidisciplinary initiative; Wildfire XPRIZE finalist and $100,000 Special Recognition awardee (Space-Based Detection and Intelligence Track).

Live demo video (YouTube) · XPRIZE Wildfire awardees · LSU News, Sept. 2026 · LSU “We Build Teams That Win”

I am a core member of the DeepFire research team at Louisiana State University, a finalist and Special Recognition Awardee ($100,000, Operational Deliverable) in the $11M Wildfire XPRIZE (Space-Based Detection and Intelligence Track), one of five Track A awardees among more than 300 teams.

XPRIZE Wildfire award ceremony, the DeepFire Special Recognition trophy, and the awardee announcement in Times Square.
  • Contributed to DeepFire, an AI platform with linked models for fire-risk prediction, early fire detection, and fire spread forecasting.
  • Developed machine learning models integrating meteorological data, satellite imagery, smoke detection (computer vision), and spatio-temporal fire spread modeling.
  • Designed data fusion pipelines combining predictive risk maps with real-time environmental signals to support proactive resource allocation for fire management agencies.
  • Contributed to a NASA ROSES-2025 A.13 proposal (submitted October 2026, under review) that extends DeepFire toward an operational decision-support system for the San Bernardino County Fire Protection District.
