I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at **Louisiana State University** (expected July 2027), working as a Graduate Research Assistant under Dr. Supratik Mukhopadhyay. My research develops, applies, and benchmarks deep learning methods for protein and other biological data, including **protein-protein interaction prediction**, protein embeddings from pretrained sequence and structure models, **regulatory genomics**, and **computational drug discovery**, with a focus on rigorous benchmarking and reproducible, openly released research software.

I am first author of eight published peer-reviewed papers, including the [DeepDrug Protein Embeddings Bank (DPEB)](https://doi.org/10.1093/nargab/lqag042) in *NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics* (2026). I also co-developed the out-of-distribution detection methods XOOD ([Best Paper, ICANN 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-04558-4_42)) and COMBOOD, and contribute machine learning and data fusion models to **DeepFire**, an LSU team that received a [Special Recognition in the Wildfire XPRIZE](https://www.xprize.org/news/xprize-wildfire-awardees). My genomics work is part of the USDA-NIFA funded [CRISP-RICE](https://www.nal.usda.gov/research-tools/food-safety-research-projects/climate-resilient-innovations-sustainable-production-rice-crisp-rice) project.

Before LSU, I earned an M.Sc. in Systems Engineering and Engineering Management from South Westphalia University of Applied Sciences in Germany and a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET. I am open to **postdoctoral research positions** in machine learning for biology, drug discovery, and environmental science.
