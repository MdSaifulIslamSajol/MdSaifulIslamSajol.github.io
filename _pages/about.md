I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at **Louisiana State University** (expected July 2027), working as a Graduate Research Assistant under Dr. Supratik Mukhopadhyay. I work with **artificial intelligence and machine learning**, including deep learning, **agentic AI**, and **large language models**, and I am interested in making these methods reliable enough to support real decisions in science. My research spans **computational biology and bioinformatics**, **computer vision**, and **wildfire detection and prevention using space-based sensor technology**.

I am first author of eight published peer-reviewed papers. In computational biology, I built the [DeepDrug Protein Embeddings Bank (DPEB)](https://doi.org/10.1093/nargab/lqag042), an open multimodal protein resource published in *NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics* (2026), and I contribute to AI for drug discovery and to genomics for climate-resilient rice in the USDA-NIFA funded [CRISP-RICE](https://www.nal.usda.gov/research-tools/food-safety-research-projects/climate-resilient-innovations-sustainable-production-rice-crisp-rice) project. In computer vision and trustworthy AI, I co-developed the out-of-distribution detection methods XOOD ([Best Paper, ICANN 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-04558-4_42)) and COMBOOD, and benchmarked deep learning models for medical image classification. In environmental AI, I contribute machine learning and data fusion models to **DeepFire**, an LSU satellite-based wildfire detection and prediction system that received a [Special Recognition in the Wildfire XPRIZE](https://www.xprize.org/news/xprize-wildfire-awardees), and I have worked on coastal hypoxia forecasting and renewable energy prediction. I have also built an AI/LLM-integrated dashboard and agentic AI-enhanced GIS maps for regional planning.

Before LSU, I earned an M.Sc. in Systems Engineering and Engineering Management from South Westphalia University of Applied Sciences in Germany and a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET. I am open to **postdoctoral research positions** in AI and machine learning for science, including biology, biomedicine, and Earth and environmental systems.
